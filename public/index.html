HITVERSE

Loud Your Speaker Volume

HitVerse is the official music platform of DAMODOT (Adedotun Adedeji Anthony), created to showcase his work, from his hit "Killaman" to his latest releases. It serves as a hub for his Afrosound, allowing him full creative control over his music without relying on external streaming platforms that might remove his work due to licensing issues. While the platform isn’t designed for revenue generation, it strengthens his connection with fans and supports his journey toward mainstream success.
